Human Code Glossary.

Explore concise explanations of important psychology and behavior concepts.

Anchoring

The tendency to rely heavily on an initial piece of information when making judgments.

Confirmation Bias

The tendency to favor information that supports an existing belief.

Cognitive Dissonance

Discomfort that can arise when beliefs, attitudes, or actions conflict.

Conformity

A change in behavior or judgment influenced by perceived group expectations.

Empathy

The ability to understand and respond to another person's emotional experience.

Framing Effect

The way information is presented can influence how people interpret it.

Halo Effect

A general impression can influence judgments about other traits.

Social Proof

Using the behavior or choices of others as a cue for what may be appropriate.
