LEARNING PATH

Emotional Intelligence

Develop self-awareness, emotional regulation, empathy, and social awareness.

9 lessonsSelf-pacedBeginner friendly
How to use this path

Move through the lessons in order, take notes, and test your understanding with reflection and knowledge checks.

CURRICULUM

Lessons

01

What Is Emotional Intelligence?

Explore skills involved in recognizing emotions, understanding their influence, and responding thoughtfully.

02

Self-Awareness

Practice identifying emotions, needs, values, and patterns without immediately judging them.

03

Recognizing Emotional Triggers

Notice situations and interpretations that intensify emotional reactions and identify a pause point.

04

Self-Regulation

Explore practical ways to pause, name an emotion, consider options, and choose a response aligned with your goals.

05

Empathy

Learn to understand another person’s perspective while recognizing that empathy does not require agreement.

06

Social Awareness

Pay attention to context, group dynamics, and others’ possible experiences without assuming you can read minds.

07

Emotional Boundaries

Understand how to care about others while maintaining limits around responsibility, time, and emotional energy.

08

Handling Conflict

Use emotional awareness and respectful language to address disagreement without escalating it unnecessarily.

09

Building Emotional Intelligence

Develop these skills through reflection, feedback, practice, and willingness to repair mistakes.

Educational note: Human behavior is complex. These lessons are intended to build understanding and critical thinking, not to diagnose people or provide instructions for exploiting them.
