LEARNING PATH

Behavioral Patterns

Study habits, reinforcement, avoidance, decision patterns, and behavior change.

9 lessonsSelf-pacedBeginner friendly
How to use this path

Move through the lessons in order, take notes, and test your understanding with reflection and knowledge checks.

CURRICULUM

Lessons

01

How Habits Form

Break down cues, routines, and outcomes to understand how repeated behavior can become more automatic.

02

Reinforcement

Explore how consequences can make a behavior more or less likely to happen again.

03

Reward and Punishment

Compare behavior consequences and consider why effects vary by person, timing, and context.

04

Avoidance

Understand how avoiding discomfort can provide short-term relief while sometimes maintaining fear or delay.

05

Procrastination

Examine how task difficulty, uncertainty, emotion, and immediate rewards can contribute to postponing work.

06

Conditioning

Explore how associations and consequences can shape learned responses to people, places, and situations.

07

Decision Patterns

Notice repeated choices and triggers, then identify practical ways to pause and choose more deliberately.

08

Behavior in Groups

Consider how roles, norms, identity, and social expectations influence behavior in group settings.

09

Changing Behavior

Use specific goals, manageable steps, environmental cues, feedback, and repetition to support sustainable change.

Educational note: Human behavior is complex. These lessons are intended to build understanding and critical thinking, not to diagnose people or provide instructions for exploiting them.
