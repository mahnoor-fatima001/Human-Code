LEARNING PATH

Human Psychology

Foundations of thought, emotion, motivation, memory, learning, and behavior.

9 lessonsSelf-pacedBeginner friendly
How to use this path

Move through the lessons in order, take notes, and test your understanding with reflection and knowledge checks.

CURRICULUM

Lessons

01

What Is Psychology?

Learn how psychology studies thought, emotion, motivation, and behavior using observation and evidence.

02

Conscious and Unconscious Processes

Explore deliberate awareness, automatic responses, and the mental processes that can shape everyday choices.

03

Motivation and Needs

Examine the needs, goals, rewards, and personal values that influence why people take action.

04

Emotion and Mood

Distinguish short-term emotional responses from longer-lasting moods and consider how each can affect behavior.

05

Memory and Attention

Understand how attention selects information and why memory can be selective, reconstructive, and imperfect.

06

Learning and Conditioning

Discover how experience, association, practice, and consequences can influence learned behavior.

07

Personality

Explore relatively consistent patterns in how people think, feel, and act without treating personality labels as destiny.

08

Decision Making

Review how goals, emotions, available information, uncertainty, and mental shortcuts influence decisions.

09

Human Behavior

Bring psychological ideas together to examine behavior in context rather than assuming one cause explains everything.

Educational note: Human behavior is complex. These lessons are intended to build understanding and critical thinking, not to diagnose people or provide instructions for exploiting them.
