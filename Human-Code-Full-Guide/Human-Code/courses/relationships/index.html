LEARNING PATH

Interpersonal Relationships

Understand trust, boundaries, conflict, friendship, family dynamics, and healthy relationships.

10 lessonsSelf-pacedBeginner friendly
How to use this path

Move through the lessons in order, take notes, and test your understanding with reflection and knowledge checks.

CURRICULUM

Lessons

01

Foundations of Relationships

Explore the role of communication, respect, shared expectations, and mutual effort in relationships.

02

Trust

Consider how reliability, honesty, consistency, and accountability help trust develop over time.

03

Healthy Boundaries

Learn to state limits clearly, respect others’ limits, and respond constructively when boundaries differ.

04

Attachment and Connection

Explore attachment as one lens for understanding closeness while avoiding fixed labels or assumptions about individuals.

05

Conflict

Understand how differences, unmet needs, and interpretations can lead to conflict and how repair can rebuild trust.

06

Friendships

Consider reciprocity, reliability, shared interests, and support as foundations of healthy friendship.

07

Family Relationships

Explore how family roles, history, expectations, and boundaries can influence relationships over time.

08

Healthy vs Unhealthy Dynamics

Compare patterns of respect, autonomy, and repair with recurring control, contempt, fear, or boundary violations.

09

Emotional Support

Learn to offer support through listening, validation, practical help, and respect for what the other person wants.

10

Maintaining Relationships

Explore how consistency, honest communication, shared responsibility, and repair help relationships adapt over time.

Educational note: Human behavior is complex. These lessons are intended to build understanding and critical thinking, not to diagnose people or provide instructions for exploiting them.
