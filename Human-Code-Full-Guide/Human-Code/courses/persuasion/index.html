LEARNING PATH

Persuasion

Understand how people communicate arguments, frames, stories, and requests ethically.

9 lessonsSelf-pacedBeginner friendly
How to use this path

Move through the lessons in order, take notes, and test your understanding with reflection and knowledge checks.

CURRICULUM

Lessons

01

What Makes Persuasion Work?

Explore how credibility, relevance, clarity, emotion, and evidence can affect how a message is received.

02

Framing

Learn how emphasis and wording shape what people notice while keeping the underlying facts accurate.

03

Reciprocity

Understand the social expectation to return favors and how genuine generosity differs from obligation-based pressure.

04

Social Proof

Explore how people use others’ behavior as information and why popularity is not the same as proof.

05

Authority and Expertise

Consider why expertise can matter and how to evaluate credentials, evidence, and conflicts of interest.

06

Consistency

Explore how commitments can guide behavior and why people should remain free to revise a choice when circumstances change.

07

Scarcity and Attention

Understand how limited availability can attract attention and why scarcity claims should be checked rather than rushed.

08

Storytelling

Learn how examples and narratives make information memorable while distinguishing an engaging story from strong evidence.

09

Ethical Persuasion

Use truthful claims, clear intent, balanced information, and respect for the other person’s right to say no.

Educational note: Human behavior is complex. These lessons are intended to build understanding and critical thinking, not to diagnose people or provide instructions for exploiting them.
